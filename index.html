CAFE · NeurIPS 2026 · Evaluations & Datasets Track (Poster)

From Pixels to Concepts: Do Segmentation Models Understand What They Segment?

Shuang Liang*, Zeqing Wang*, Yuxian Li*, Xihui Liu, Han Wang

A correct mask can still reflect the wrong concept. CAFE tests this with 2,146 paired examples that introduce misleading appearance, context, or material cues while preserving the target region and mask.

I co-curated the benchmark and built CAFE-SAM3, an agent that inspects regions, refines prompts, and selects a matching mask or abstains. I also led follow-up evaluation and failure analysis, with mentoring from Profs. Xihui Liu and Han Wang.
